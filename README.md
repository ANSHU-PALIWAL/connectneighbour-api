# ConnectNeighbour — Core Application Services

ConnectNeighbour is India's privacy-first, hyper-local community platform designed to help verified neighbors connect, share skills, organize local initiatives, and support one another within a unified 150 km discovery radius.

## About ConnectNeighbour

Modern neighborhoods often lack simple, private ways for residents to interact and build trusted communities. ConnectNeighbour bridges this gap by providing:

- **Verified Local Discovery:** Connect with residents in your immediate community within a 150 km radius.
- **Privacy-First Communications:** High-trust networking where personal phone numbers and exact private home coordinates are never compromised.
- **Community Collaboration:** Form hyper-local interest groups, organize neighborhood events, and share skills with verified neighbors.
- **Real-Time Neighborhood Support:** Secure direct messaging, community forums, and peer-to-peer neighborhood connections.
